Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

1. How We Treat Your Data

At Reflex Interactive, we keep data collection minimal and straightforward. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site or play our games, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

2. What We Collect

  • Contact Info You Share: If you sign up for our newsletter or contact support, we keep details like your name and email so we can reply or send studio updates.

  • Basic Technical Site Data: Standard web logs (like browser type and general location) to help us ensure the site runs smoothly and stays secure.

  • In-Game Metrics: Anonymous gameplay data (like crash reports or level completion rates) to help us balance mechanics and fix bugs.

3. We Don't Sell Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted operational tools (such as web hosting or newsletter delivery platforms) that help us run the studio, or if required by law enforcement.

4. Your Data Rights

Under UK GDPR regulations, you hold full control over your personal data. At any time, you can ask to see what information we have about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete your personal data completely.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website and online tools are not intended for kids under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal info to us, drop us a line and we will delete it promptly.
