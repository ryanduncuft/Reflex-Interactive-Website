Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who we are

Reflex Interactive is an independent game studio based in Manchester, United Kingdom. This policy explains how we use personal information when you visit our website, contact us, buy a game, or order merchandise. For privacy questions or requests, email legal@reflexinteractive.com.

2. Information we handle

  • Information you provide when contacting support, subscribing to updates, or placing an order, such as your name, email address, message, and delivery address for physical goods.
  • Order details such as the products and prices purchased, order status, and a payment reference used to fulfil and support the transaction.
  • Technical information in standard hosting and security logs, such as IP address, browser, request time, and pages requested.
  • Game diagnostics only where a particular game collects them; any additional collection is described in that game's own notice.

Do not send payment card numbers or security codes to us by email or support form.

3. Payments and Stripe

When you choose to buy a game or merchandise, checkout is provided by Stripe. Stripe handles the payment details you enter on its checkout page under its own privacy terms. We do not receive or store your full card number or card security code. We receive the transaction and product information needed to confirm payment, provide support, and fulfil the order. For physical orders, the delivery address is used to arrange shipping.

Stripe may process information as an independent controller for some of its services. Review Stripe's privacy policy for more detail.

4. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

The Settings panel stores your reduced-motion and data-saver choices in your browser's local storage under the key reflex.settings.v1. These preferences stay on that device and are not sent to us.

Our hosting and security providers may use necessary technical storage to deliver and protect the site. The not-found page also loads a Google Ads measurement tag, which may use cookies or similar identifiers. You can manage or clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect provider features or measurement.

5. How we use and share information

We use information to operate and secure the site, respond to messages, process and support orders, deliver physical goods, meet accounting and legal duties, and understand advertising referrals where the measurement tag is present. We share only what is reasonably needed with providers such as Stripe, site hosting, delivery services, and communications providers, or where the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including customer support, order records, security, and legal or accounting requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain uses of your personal information, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. To make a request, contact legal@reflexinteractive.com. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law requires or permits it.

8. Children and policy updates

Our website and store are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. We may update this notice as our services change; the date above shows when it was last revised.
