About Reflex Interactive

Founded in Manchester in 2022, Reflex Interactive builds focused action games with clear systems, atmospheric worlds, and a practical approach to production.

Our Mission, Values, and Team

Mission

Create memorable games through responsive mechanics, strong atmosphere, and consistent attention to the player experience.

Values

Clarity
Communicating plainly and building systems players can trust.
Craft
Improving feel, readability, and reliability through iteration.
Focus
Choosing sustainable scope and finishing work properly.

Team

Reflex Interactive is a small studio combining design, engineering, art, and production around a shared standard for polished play.

Our Promise

We keep our work focused, communicate clearly, and build games around responsive systems, strong atmosphere, and respect for the player's time.
