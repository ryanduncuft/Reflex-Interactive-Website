About Reflex Interactive

Founded in Manchester in 2022, we strive to build fun experiences for our players through responsive mechanics, strong atmosphere, and consistent attention to the player experience. We keep our work focused, communicate clearly, and build games around responsive systems, strong atmosphere, and respect for the player's time.

Missions

Create memorable games our players can enjoy, and build a studio that is sustainable, clear, and focused on craft.

Values

Clarity
Communicating plainly and building systems players can trust.
Craft

Focus
Choosing sustainable scope and finishing work properly.

Studio

Reflex Interactive is a small studio focusing on narrative action, precision gameplay, and atmospheric worlds. We are based in Manchester, United Kingdom, and have been operating since 2022.
