Shipping & Returns

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Digital PC games

Digital games are supplied electronically, not shipped. Check the title's listed operating system and system requirements before purchase. You are responsible for confirming your PC, operating system, available storage, and required internet connection are suitable. We cannot guarantee performance on every hardware or software configuration.

For an online purchase of digital content, a 14-day cancellation period normally applies. If you ask us to begin supplying a download or key during that period, the cancellation right ends only after we have obtained your express consent to immediate supply and your acknowledgement that the right to cancel will be lost when supply begins, and provided the required confirmation is sent on a durable medium. Where those steps have been completed and the content has been supplied or activated, we do not offer a change-of-mind refund. If they have not, your statutory cancellation right may still apply.

This policy does not remove your rights if digital content is faulty, not as described, or otherwise fails to meet applicable consumer-law requirements. Contact support@reflexinteractive.com with your order reference and a description of the issue.

Physical merchandise and apparel: shipping

Physical orders are fulfilled from Great Britain. Our current Stripe checkout accepts delivery addresses in Great Britain (GB) only. Shipping charges and any available delivery estimates are shown at checkout before payment. Please check your address carefully; contact us promptly if you notice an error.

We will dispatch orders within the timeframe shown at checkout or in your order confirmation. If a delivery is delayed or has not arrived, contact support@reflexinteractive.com with your order reference so we can investigate with the carrier.

Physical merchandise and apparel: returns

In addition to your statutory rights, we offer a 30-day return period from delivery for physical merchandise. For a change-of-mind return, items must be unworn, unwashed, unused, and returned in their original condition with tags and packaging where provided. Contact us within 30 days of delivery; we will send return instructions and the correct return address. Please do not send an item before receiving those instructions.

For a change-of-mind return, you are responsible for the direct cost of return postage. If an item is faulty, damaged on arrival, or not what you ordered, contact us first; we will provide an appropriate remedy and cover reasonable return costs where required.

For statutory online-order cancellation, tell us within 14 days after the day the goods are delivered, then send the goods back within 14 days after telling us. We may wait to refund until we receive the goods or you provide evidence that they have been sent back. Refunds are made using the original payment method in accordance with applicable law. A deduction may be made where the value of an item has been reduced by handling beyond what is needed to inspect it.

How to request help or a return

Email support@reflexinteractive.com with your order number, the item, and whether you are requesting a change-of-mind return or reporting a problem. We will reply with the next steps. Keep proof of postage until your return is complete.

These terms do not limit any consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. See the Consumer Contracts Regulations cancellation periods and digital-content cancellation rules.
